  The reasoning was: BTC/ETH are the two most efficient, most-arbitraged markets in crypto, so net of fees their direction is ~random. Less-efficient mid-caps (#9–15) are where trends
  are most likely to persist long enough to be tradeable — so they're the best place to start. The meme names swing hard (good for a volatility signal) but are the easiest to overfit.

  And the two levers that matter more than the ticker:
  - Timeframe — 1h is the most efficient; try 4h / 1d where autocorrelation is higher.
  - Min predicted move — require the forecast to clear a real % move so fees don't eat the edge.

  This is exactly what the new Batch Runner is for: import a handful of these, then run one batch across all of them and look at the median excess vs buy & hold rather than any single
  coin. Want me to kick off the background imports for, say, the 7 mid-caps at 4h + 1d so you can batch them?
